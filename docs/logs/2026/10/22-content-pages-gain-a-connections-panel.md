Content pages gain a Connections panel with incoming links, their source section, heading and link text, and a Local graph link. The graph view accepts `focus` and `depth`, selects that node and uses the existing neighbourhood focus, and offers Show all. `design/graph-evolution.md` records the roadmap and guardrails; `usage/graph.md`, `reference/graph.md`, `reference/themes.md`, `design/branding.md` and `design/rendering.md` say so.
