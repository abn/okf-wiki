A `--nav-links` flag (and `OKF_WIKI_NAV_LINKS`), plus a `nav-links` action input, adds links to the header as `Label=URL` pairs. An external target opens in a new tab and is marked with an arrow; an in-bundle path resolves as a body link and opens in the same tab. On a phone they move into the drawer, the in-wiki links first under "On this wiki" and the rest under "Elsewhere".
