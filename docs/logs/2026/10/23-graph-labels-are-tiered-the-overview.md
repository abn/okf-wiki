Graph labels are tiered. The overview names tag hubs and the busiest quarter of the connected pages; ordinary page names appear as the reader zooms in, or when a filter or focus asks for them. The status line says so at the map scale. `usage/graph.md`, `reference/graph.md`, `design/rendering.md` and `design/graph-evolution.md` record it.
