A node draws a short label instead of its whole title. The catalogue carries `label` when a title needs shortening, and the view uses it for the canvas name, the list row, the details heading, the status line, the connection buttons, and the path picker, while the full title stays on hover, in the details panel, and on the page. `reference/graph.md` and `usage/graph.md` record the field and the rule.
