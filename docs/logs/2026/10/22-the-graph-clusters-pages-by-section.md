The graph can cluster pages by top-level section in a labelled grid, with tag hubs and missing pages in their own cells. Tag hubs can be collapsed without removing tag filtering, and Show Tags is disabled while they are hidden. The controls clear focus and relayout. `usage/graph.md`, `reference/graph.md`, `design/rendering.md`, `reference/themes.md`, `usage/offline.md` and `design/graph-evolution.md` say so.
