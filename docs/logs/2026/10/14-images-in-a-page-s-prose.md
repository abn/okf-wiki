Images in a page's prose open in the same viewer as diagrams, with pan and zoom. Clicking one enlarges it and the viewer scales it to fit; the brand mark and other chrome stay out of it. The `wiki-lbx-pill` now reads Image rather than Diagram there, and the docs record it under `reference/themes.md`.
