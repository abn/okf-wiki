The sidebar's third level reads as a group's leaves. A page whose title repeats its group heading drops the shared prefix, so the row fits one line at any rail width, the pages hang off a hairline rule rather than a deeper indent, and a section opens one group at a time so a rail of many groups stays short. A page can override its rail label with `nav` in frontmatter. `design/rendering.md` and `reference/themes.md` say so.
