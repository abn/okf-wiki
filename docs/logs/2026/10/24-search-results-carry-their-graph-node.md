Search results carry their graph node ID. The selected hit can open in the graph with `focus` and the search query in `q`, so the hit is selected and other matches stay lit while non-matches fade. Tag-search rows, which have no page node, hide the action. `usage/graph.md`, `reference/themes.md` and `design/rendering.md` record it.
