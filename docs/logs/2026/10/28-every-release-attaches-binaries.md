Every release attaches static binaries for linux, macOS and Windows on amd64 and arm64. Each archive holds the binary, the license, the README, the third-party notices and the mermaid runtime, and `checksums.txt` covers the set. `usage/cli.md`, `README.md` and `contribution/index.md` record it, and `adr/0005-release-binaries.md` records the decision.
