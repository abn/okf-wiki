Heading permalinks cover subheadings. Every level from two to six grows the `#` link on hover, not only the level-two sections, and every level takes the scroll margin that lands a deep-link jump below the sticky bars. Headings that label an app widget opt out. `design/rendering.md`, `reference/themes.md` and `testbed/headings.md` record it.
