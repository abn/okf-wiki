status: stable
---

The Linked from list in the Connections panel ran the source title, its
section, the heading and the quoted link texts together on one line. Each source
now takes two lines. The title leads the first, with the section as a small
label on the right, left out when it repeats the title. The second holds the
heading, marked with `#`, and the link texts, separated by dots. A source that
links from several places keeps its nested rows, with the link count on the
second line. [Graph view](../../../usage/graph.md) describes the panel.
