status: stable
---

On a page without a type or tags, Download Markdown sat alone on its own row as a
pill pushed to the right, leaving a gap that read as a hole with a button in it.
With no chips beside it, it now drops the pill and becomes a small text link with
its icon, at the start of the row and pulled up under the lead. Hovering
underlines it. On a phone it keeps a 44px touch target. Pages with a type chip
or tags keep the pill at the end of the row.
[Rendering](../../../design/rendering.md) describes both forms.
