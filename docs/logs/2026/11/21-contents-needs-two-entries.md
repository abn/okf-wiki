The table of contents, in the rail, the dropdown and the phone button, now
renders only when a page has at least two level-two headings. A page with one
heading showed a contents list that only repeated it. Nothing fills the empty
rail: the third column stays, so the reading column keeps its position as a
reader moves between pages. Below the wide breakpoint the breadcrumb gets the
whole bar. [Rendering](../../../design/rendering.md) and the template contract
in [Branding](../../../design/branding.md) describe the rule.
