title: "Empty connections are hidden on log entries, one line elsewhere"
description: "A log entry nothing links to drops its Connections section, and any other page with no backlinks shows a single line with the Local graph link."
tags: [log, update]
kind: Update
status: stable
---

Every log entry ended in the same empty Connections block, a full heading over
"No pages link here yet." A log entry that nothing links to now has no
Connections section, because its Older and Newer cards are how a reader moves
through the log. Any other page with no backlinks shows one ruled line, "No other
page links here yet.", with the Local graph link on the right and no heading.
Pages with backlinks keep the full panel. [Graph view](../../../usage/graph.md)
describes both cases.
