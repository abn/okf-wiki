Added a `testbed/` section that exercises the whole rendering surface on viewable pages: [callouts](../../../testbed/callouts.md) at all five levels, [blocks](../../../testbed/blocks.md) with tables, task lists and footnotes, [code](../../../testbed/code.md) across lexers and one language with none, four [diagrams](../../../testbed/diagrams.md) including an invalid one, and [headings](../../../testbed/headings.md) that repeat and collide on purpose.
